Schedule Settings

Changes apply to all calculations, including past entries. "Remind after" flags a day when you've clocked in but not out for longer than this. Reduced hours for a date range, such as Ramadan or summer schedules. These override the standard hours above for the dates they cover. The Ramadan button fills in the expected dates and a 6-hour day — confirm them once the moon sighting is announced, since the start can shift by a day.
