Settings & Profile

Profile

Your name and photo. Both are visible to your whole team, and both are yours alone to change.

Your profile

Working Hours & Schedule

The week this person is measured against — which days they work, how long a day is, and when it normally starts.

Daily Target

Saving recalculates every day already logged, not just days from here on.

“Remind after” flags a day when you’ve clocked in but not out for longer than this. “Grace period” is how late you can arrive before a day counts as a late arrival. “Annual Leave Days” is the yearly entitlement used to calculate the Leave Balance card.

Log Highlighting

Which clock times the Log paints red. This changes nothing about the hours themselves — only how they are marked.

Only affects the Log tab, which marks a clock time red when it's outside your standard hours by more than the grace period. The Shortfall tab ignores clock times entirely — it only cares whether the hours got made up.

Seasonal Hours

Reduced hours for a date range. These override the standard hours for the dates they cover.

The Ramadan button fills in the expected dates and a 6-hour day — confirm them once the moon sighting is announced, since the start can shift by a day.

Appearance

How the app looks on this device. Saved in this browser, not to your account — so a phone can sit in dark while the desk stays light.

Notifications

Whether this browser gets a real notification — even with the app closed — for your own forgotten-clock-out reminder and anything an admin sends.

People

Role changes take effect immediately. Deactivating blocks sign-in but keeps attendance history; deleting removes the person and every entry they logged, and can't be undone.

No one matches that filter.

Organisation Defaults

The schedule a brand-new account starts with. Until it is set, new employees silently inherit Sunday–Thursday 08:00–16:00, and every figure they see is measured against the wrong week. Saving does not change anyone's existing schedule.

Daily Target
Seasonal Hours

Reduced hours every new account starts with. Anyone can still override them in their own Schedule Settings.

Company-Wide Days

Mark a public holiday or company-wide day off for the whole team in one action, instead of entering it person by person. Days that already hold recorded hours are listed before anything is written, and skipped unless you explicitly choose to overwrite them.

Announcement & Sign-Up

A message across the top of the app for everyone, and whether strangers can create their own account.

No organisation default schedule is set — anyone who signs up will start on the fallback Sun–Thu, 8h week instead of your actual hours.

Notifications

A real push notification, even to a closed app — to everyone, or to specific people you pick below. Only reaches someone who has turned it on in their own Settings.
